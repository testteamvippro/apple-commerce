## ⚠️ Important Reminders

```
SAFETY:
  🔒 Keep passwords secure
  🔒 Don't share admin access
  🔒 Use strong passwords
  🔒 Change password regularly

BACKUPS:
  💾 Download backup weekly
  💾 Store in 2-3 locations
  💾 Keep at least 4 weeks of backups
  💾 Test restore once per month

SUPPORT:
  📞 Contact developer early
  📞 Don't panic if something breaks
  📞 Data is backed up and recoverable
  📞 Ask questions - that's OK!

SCHEDULE:
  📅 Daily: 5-10 minutes
  📅 Weekly: 30 minutes (Sunday)
  📅 Monthly: 1-2 hours (1st of month)
  📅 Quarterly: 2 hours (every 3 months)
```
